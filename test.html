01Fixed first, sealed first

Most thesis dashboards pick their numbers after seeing the data. This one did the opposite: six claims with hard numeric thresholds were written down first, the file was hashed with SHA-256, and that hash was sealed in a NEAR testnet contract that can only ever accept it once. Only then is anything tested. If a claim fails below, nobody quietly edits the threshold — the sealed hash would stop matching, and this page would show it.

02The live test

Press run. Your browser fetches live figures from public, keyless APIs (DefiLlama, CoinGecko, NEAR mainnet RPC) and judges each claim against the threshold that was sealed above. Nothing is fetched from a server of ours, and a claim whose source cannot be reached is marked UNVERIFIABLE — never guessed, never quietly dropped.

03The bear case

  
The counter-evidence, stated as plainly as the claims:

  

    
Fees are thin. NEAR's chain fees over the last 30 days come in far below the
      $250K bar set in claim C3. Whatever the Intents economy is doing, it is not yet paying the chain
      much in direct fees — the "usage will pay for the chain" story is, today, unproven.

    
Price has not recovered. NEAR trades roughly 84% below its all-time high, so
      claim C6 fails outright. Market value (C5) clears its bar on the back of supply and rank, not on
      price strength — a large market cap and a deeply-drawn-down price are both true at once.

    
TVL clears — barely. Claim C2 passes by only a few percent. A bad week in DeFi
      markets flips it, and a thesis that depends on a 3.5% margin is not a strong thesis.

    
Two claims failing tonight is not a bug in this page. It is the system working as designed:
      the thresholds were fixed before the data was seen, so the data gets the last word.
